Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026 · Covers: the Alfred AI - Handsfree System iPhone app and Handsfree AI Helper (Mac) · Operated by SeoulFactory · The Korean version of this policy is the original; this is a translation.

1. Information we collect

Alfred AI - Handsfree System has no sign-up and runs no servers of its own. Audio recordings, transcribed text, AI summaries, settings and the API keys you enter are stored only on your device. The developer cannot access this information.

2. Sending to third-party AI services

Only after you agree in the app the first time you use an AI feature, the data below is sent directly to that company with your own API key.

  • Anthropic (Claude): transcribed text, recording transcripts and questions — for chat answers, polishing, summaries, translation, speaker labels and search
  • OpenAI: transcribed text and questions — for chat answers / Speak and Record audio — for transcription (if you choose ChatGPT transcription in Settings)
  • Apple speech recognition: if you use the iPhone's built-in speech recognition, your voice may be processed by Apple.

Each company handles data under its own privacy policy.

3. Sending to your laptop and talking with the mouse (Handsfree AI Helper)

When you send to your laptop, the transcribed text goes straight to your Mac over your local Wi-Fi and never passes through the internet. The Mac helper stores only a random token on the Mac to recognize paired iPhones. It pastes the text it receives into the text field of the app you chose and does not keep it. With “Talk with the mouse”, the helper listens through the Mac microphone only while you hold the double-clicked mouse button, transcribes with Apple speech recognition on the Mac, and pastes the text at your cursor. Audio and text are not stored. The troubleshooting log (~/Library/Logs/HandsfreeAIHelper.log) records only times and character counts, never the content.

4. Permissions

  • iPhone: Microphone (recording and dictation), Speech Recognition (built-in dictation), Local Network (finding and sending to your laptop)
  • Mac: Accessibility (pasting into text fields, sending, detecting mouse buttons), Microphone and Speech Recognition (talking with the mouse)

We do not use screen recording, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you.

5. Your rights

In the iPhone app, go to Settings → Privacy to withdraw consent for AI sending or delete all data immediately. In the Mac helper window, “Unpair all” removes pairing information. Deleting the app also deletes the data on the device.

6. Children

This service is not directed to children under 14.

7. Contact

Reach us on the GitHub issues page. If this policy changes, we will post it here with a new effective date.
